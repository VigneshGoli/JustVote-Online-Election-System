About JustVote

Classical Election System

Classical election systems have several drawbacks compared to online voting systems

  • Time Consuming

  • Cost Ineffective

  • Inconvenient

  • Less Voter Participation

Security Concerns

Classical election systems face security risks like duplicate voting and bribery, undermining fairness and transparency. These vulnerabilities demand modernization to ensure the integrity of democratic processes. JustVote helps to eradicate these issues in the Old-fashioned voting by digitalizing the voting mechanism. This way, by integrating proper security algorithms we can completely implement safe and fair elections.

Vision

To revolutionize the electoral process by creating a secure, accessible, and transparent online platform that empowers citizens to participate in democratic elections from anywhere in the world.

Mission

Our mission is to develop a secure and user-friendly online election system using web development technologies. We prioritize accessibility, reliability, and inclusivity to promote greater voter engagement and trust in the democratic process.
